## English (en-US)

**App name:** WallRush — Wall Race
**Short title:** WallRush

**Short description (≤80):**
Race to the far side and wall your rivals in! Fast, strategic, offline board game.

**Full description:**

🧱 **WallRush** — a complete strategic board game in your pocket!

Race your rival to the opposite side of the board and block their path with walls. Easy to learn in seconds, deep enough for hours!

**How to play:**
• Each turn: move one step OR place a wall
• First to reach the far side wins
• 10 walls per player — use them wisely!
• Jump over an adjacent rival
• Fair play guaranteed: a path to the goal always remains

**Features:**
🤖 Smart AI with 3 difficulty levels (easy/medium/hard) — Minimax with Alpha-Beta pruning
⚡ Blitz mode: 10 seconds per turn
🎯 Up to 4 players on one device (friends & family)
🌍 8 full languages: English, العربية, Español, Français, Deutsch, Português, Italiano, Türkçe
🎨 Cozy "board-game club" design — warm colors and clean typography
💡 Hints, undo and path guide for beginners
📊 Local stats: wins, games, win streak
📴 100% offline — local device profiles only, no ads, zero data collected
📳 Optional haptics and synthesized sound effects

A family-friendly game for all ages — download now and play your first duel!
