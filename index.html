How to play

  • 🏁Reach the opposite side before your rival.
  • 👣Each turn: move one step OR place a wall.
  • 🧱Walls block everyone. You have a limited stock.
  • 🦘Jump over an adjacent rival. If blocked behind, jump sideways.
  • 🚫You can never fully block a path to the goal.
  • 👆Tap a glowing cell to move. Tap a groove to build a wall.
